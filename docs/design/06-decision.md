# The decision: what we're building

*The architect's consolidation of the design debate, and the spec the implementation
follows. The debate itself is in 00–05. Both analysts signed off on every row of
`05-resolution.md`.*

## How the debate concluded

| Round | What happened |
|---|---|
| 1. Proposals | **Analyst A** (Experience & Brand) proposed **"Limelight"**: the living room as a private cinema, with lime as the only light, and the date ending in an "Admit Two" ticket. **Analyst B** (Product, Accessibility & Feasibility) proposed **"Couch-Proof"**: one decision per screen, honest data and a font budget. |
| 2. Cross-critique | The analysts persuaded each other so thoroughly that they **swapped sides on six questions**: brand hue, serif, swiping, moods, photo shape and poster grid. They converged on the velvet page, link-plus-text sharing, the SVG wordmark and four MUSTs. |
| 3. Resolution | A proposed a middle ground for each of the 10 open items. B accepted 9 and countered 1: how the Fraunces font loads, since `optional` in a single-page app means first visits never see it. A accepted the counter. **All 10 items were agreed, so no architect tie-break was needed.** |

**One open dependency: the product owner's sign-off on the lime hue.** Zest **#9FE143** replaces the heritage **#23D160** for UI fills. The heritage green lives on as the rind of the lime-slice logo. Reverting means changing the `--lime-*` block in `assets/css/tokens.css`, and the contrast test will then say which pairings need adjusting.

## Design language: "Limelight × Couch-Proof"

**Principles**
1. **Lights down, lime up.** Velvet surfaces do the quiet work. Lime marks the one next thing, and it's the only vivid color on screen.
2. **Two people, one phone.** Touch targets are at least 48px, type is readable at arm's length, and the primary action sits in a sticky bar within thumb reach.
3. **Defaults, not dead ends.** Every form can be submitted as it is ("Any" is pre-selected), every empty state offers a way to loosen the filters, and every error has a retry.
4. **Honest data.** We never invent cook times, difficulty or servings, never say "allergy-safe", and show real certifications only. Heuristics are labeled as heuristics.
5. **Accessible by construction.** Native elements come first. WCAG 2.2 AA is the floor, measured in CI. Reduced motion is honored. Color is never the only signal.
6. **Light on the phone.** No framework, 82.5 KB of fonts, one API call before first results, lazy images, and every response cached.

**Voice:** warm, witty, a little flirty, never crude. Wordplay goes in headlines only, buttons are plain verbs, and we say "you two" or "your date", never gendered roles. Errors drop the jokes after the headline.

### Color

Two themes: **Late Show** (dark, velvet) and **Matinee** (light, program paper).

- **Default is "Auto":** dark when the device is dark **or** it's 18:00–06:00 locally, otherwise light. It's applied before first paint.
- There's a Lights up/down toggle in the header, and an Auto / Dark / Light radio group in the footer and on the House Rules page.
- `prefers-contrast: more` promotes secondary and muted text to primary, and `forced-colors` falls back to system colors.

### Typography (82.5 KB of Latin; Latin-Ext loads only when a page needs it)

| Face | Use | Bytes |
|---|---|---|
| Roboto Condensed 800, **outlined to SVG** | the dateLime wordmark (the logo is unchanged, it just costs no font file) | 0 |
| Fraunces, upright, wght axis only | **H1 only**, the voice of each screen. Preloaded with `swap` over a size-adjusted serif fallback, and precached by the service worker | 36.6 KB |
| Roboto Condensed 700 | H2, H3, labels and ticket fields. Uppercase only through `text-transform`, for labels of three words or fewer | 21.1 KB |
| Atkinson Hyperlegible Next 400/600 | body and UI text. Its 1/l/I and 0/O can't be confused, which matters for free-text measures like "1 l" | 24.8 KB |

The text floor is 0.875rem. Line length is 68ch or less.

### Shape, motion, imagery

- **Rectangles are screens, circles are plates, pills are actions.**
  - Posters are always 2:3.
  - Food and drink thumbnails of 160px or less are circles. The full square appears in the recipe sheet and at the top of the Cook-along.
  - The ticket is the only notched object.
- **Radii:** 4, 8, 12, 20 and 28px, plus pill. **Spacing:** a 4-point scale.
- **Motion:**
  - Step transitions take 300ms or less, using View Transitions where the browser supports them.
  - The ticket "prints" once, in 650ms.
  - "It's a date" plays when the shortlists match.
  - The lime moon rises once per session behind the landing wordmark.
  - Everything is removed under `prefers-reduced-motion`.
  - No animated box-shadows, and no large blurs.
- **Missing posters** become typographic posters. **Missing photos** become the plate color. Image-host size suffixes fall back to the original automatically.

## Features: final priority and acceptance criteria

### MUST

1. **Date Ticket ("Admit Two").**
   - Save it (and undo). Share it as a link plus complete text: Web Share, falling back to the clipboard, then to a selectable field.
   - An `.ics` file in floating local time with a VALARM reminder.
   - A run of show (start cooking, eat, press play, credits) with cook-time chips for 30, 45, 60 or 90 minutes, labelled "your estimate".
   - A "You've been sent a date" banner and **Save to my dates** for the recipient.
   - Diary export and import as JSON.
2. **Vibe Pairing.**
   - Three tiers: origin (country or language), then TMDB keywords, then genre.
   - Diets intersect by `idMeal`, with an announced staged fallback.
   - A "Why?" line and a "Match the movie" switch.
   - The drink is chosen on the ticket and defaults to zero-proof (zero-proof, a lime cocktail, or no drink).
3. **House Rules.**
   - Streaming services and region: one Discover request, with JustWatch credit.
   - A US rating cap, using `certification_country` and `certification.lte`.
   - Vegetarian, vegan or pescatarian.
   - The default drink, and "Match the movie".
   - All remembered on this device.
4. **Cook-along.**
   - Wake Lock with a visible off switch and a notice when it isn't supported.
   - Step cards, an "All steps" view and an ingredient checklist.
   - Timers found in the step text, based on timestamps, with a chime, vibration and an announcement.
   - 64px Back and Next buttons, plus the ← and → keys.
   - A countdown to "Press play".
   - Works offline for saved dates.
5. **Surprise us / Spin the Lime.**
   - The movie comes from Discover, with House Rules, a quality floor and a random page.
   - The meal comes from the Vibe Pairing pool, not `random.php`.
   - The drink follows the House Rule.
   - Hold toggles (`aria-pressed`), one summary announcement, and it lands on the ticket.

### SHOULD

1. **Match Night, as a blind shortlist.**
   - Each person hearts up to 3 movies, and the first person's hearts are hidden during the hand-off.
   - Overlaps get "It's a date".
   - With no overlap, the second person picks from all hearted movies (6 at most).
   - A 4th heart announces "3 of 3" instead of disabling anything.
2. **Mood labels and curated blends.** Rom-com is 35 AND 10749, Scary-funny is 27 AND 35. If House Rules shrink a blend below 8 results, it relaxes AND to OR and says so.
3. **Best-effort ingredient scan** for foods to avoid (pork, beef, shellfish, nuts, dairy, mushrooms). It uses synonym lists and runs only on recipes we've looked up. It's labelled "We check ingredient lists only".

### COULD

- A typographic share image with no third-party pixels.
- Afterglow: rate a past date and add notes.
- A "their mood" second group.

### WON'T (v1)

- A swipe deck.
- A two-phone match relay.
- Share images that include photos (CORS is unverified).
- Invented cook times, difficulty or servings.
- "Allergy-safe" badges.
- Accounts or cloud sync.
- Push reminders.
- LLM mood matching.
- Autoplaying video.
- Infinite scroll.
- Bulk-downloading TheMealDB.
- "Play on Netflix" deep links.

## Architect's implementation notes

These are refinements within the agreed decisions, not overrides:

- **Header theme control.** The header carries a two-state *Lights up/down* toggle (`aria-pressed`). The full three-state **Auto / Dark / Light** radio group, which B asked for so that Auto can always be picked again, lives in the footer and on House Rules. Three 48px options don't fit a 390px header next to the logo and "Your dates" without crowding.
- **Match Night ships as a SHOULD** so that the five-couple hallway test the analysts asked for can run on the real thing. The README flags it as awaiting that validation.
- **Recipe cards show their source** ("French", "Dessert"). It comes from the list the recipe came from, so no lookup per card is needed. Details load only when a recipe sheet opens.
- **"Match the movie" off** switches the recipe step to a category picker (Chicken, Pasta, Seafood and so on).
